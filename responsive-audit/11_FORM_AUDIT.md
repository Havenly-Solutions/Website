# 11 Form Usability & Field Audit

- **Touch Target Height**: All inputs, selects, textareas, and submit buttons meet or exceed $44\\text{px}$ min-height.
- **Input Font Size**: $16\\text{px}$ font size on mobile prevents mobile WebKit auto-zooming.
- **Grid Fields (`.row2`)**: 1-column on mobile, 2-column on tablet/desktop.
